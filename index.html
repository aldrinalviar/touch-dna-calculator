Visual Transfer Pathway Builder

Build a sequence of DNA transfer, recovery, surface-retention, or laboratory-loss events. Surface-retention values represent DNA that remains on the surface and is therefore treated as unavailable for downstream recovery. Swab retention and extraction loss are classified as sampling losses and are also subtracted from the amount entering that step. Transfer and recovery values multiply by the stated fraction. Use this as an exploratory model only.
